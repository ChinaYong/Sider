# Sider Privacy Policy

Updated: October 31, 2026

Sider for Microsoft Edge lets you add selected web content and prompt presets to AI websites you choose.

Data: Sider processes open-tab titles/URLs, selected text, requested page content, prompts, AI conversation excerpts and chosen attachments. These may contain personal information. Settings are stored locally; temporary references use browser session storage or memory.

Sharing: When you insert, send or attach material, it is provided to the selected AI website. Attachments may upload before you send a message. Those websites handle accounts, connections and submitted data under their own policies.

Session compatibility: In embedded ChatGPT pages, Sider reads JavaScript-accessible cookies and account/session markers already provided by ChatGPT. Values remain in that page; extension diagnostics report fallback flags, not the values. Sider cannot read HttpOnly cookies.

Sider has no developer-operated data collection server, advertising trackers or data sales. Limited loading/error diagnostics stay in the browser.

Access: Other-site permissions are requested by origin. Scoped session rules remove CSP and X-Frame-Options headers only from registered, authorized AI-site subframes inside Sider.

Controls: Choose or deselect references, revoke site permissions, disable features or uninstall Sider. Uninstalling removes extension-managed local data. Delete exported files separately. Manage AI-site data through that website. We update this policy when practices change.

Contact: [Sider Issues](https://github.com/ChinaYong/Sider/issues). This is public; do not post sensitive information.
